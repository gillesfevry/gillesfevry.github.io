Welcome to my webpage!

I am a PhD student at [CERMICS](https://cermics-lab.enpc.fr/), École Nationale des Ponts et Chaussées (starting October 30, 2026), supervised by [Pierre-Cyril Aubin-Frankowski](https://pcaubin.github.io/) and [Axel Parmentier](https://cermics.enpc.fr/~parmenta/), and co-supervised by [Yohann De Castro](https://ydecastro.github.io/). I work on generative models for combinatorial optimization.

Before that, I graduated from [ENSAE Paris](https://www.ensae.fr/) and from the [MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) master in Machine Learning at [ENS Paris-Saclay](https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/).
